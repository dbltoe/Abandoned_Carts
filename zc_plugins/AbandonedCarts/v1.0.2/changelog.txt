Abandoned Carts -- changelog
============================

v1.0.2
------
- New: Ask Guests Before Reminding? (off). When on, One Page
  Checkout shows guests an "Email me a reminder if I leave items in
  my cart." checkbox; only guests who tick it are reminded.
- Every reminder says it's an advertisement from your store
  (CAN-SPAM).
- Nothing is kept about a shopper your settings say can't be
  emailed (for example a guest with E-Mail Guests? off); such
  records are deleted at the next scheduler run.
- The readme covers Australia beside the EU, UK and Canada.

v1.0.1
------
- Plugin Manager and the readme link the Abandoned Carts support
  thread on the Zen Cart forum.
- Fixed: the readme said Abandoned Carts Pro puts its coupon in the
  last reminder. Pro puts it in the reminder you choose.
- Upgrade in Plugin Manager; no code, settings, data or email change.

v1.0.0
------
The first release (2026-10-05).

- Records a cart once the shopper can be emailed: a customer with an
  account, or a One Page Checkout guest who saved their contact details.
  Anonymous carts are never stored.
- One automatic reminder per cart, a set number of hours after the last
  cart change, sent by a scheduler run from cron every 15 minutes.
- Checks before each send: too old, unsubscribed, ordered since (any
  payment path), guest and newsletter-only settings, minimum value, and a
  wait while the shopper is still browsing.
- Return to Your Cart restores the exact cart; one-click unsubscribe;
  your postal address on every email.
- Customers > Abandoned Carts: scheduler health, a 30-day summary, every
  cart's items and history, Stop Reminders.
- Records deleted after Keep Cart Records days.
- Preview Email definition for the reminder.
